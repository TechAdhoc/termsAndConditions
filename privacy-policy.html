About CoinDribble

CoinDribble is a personal finance app made by TechAdhoc. It reads your bank SMS messages to automatically track your spending — all on your phone, with no internet connection needed.

This page explains what information the app uses, why it needs it, and how your privacy is protected. If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to us.

CoinDribble works completely offline. Your financial information never leaves your phone.
🚫

What We Do Not Collect

CoinDribble never collects or sees any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or home address
  • Your bank account numbers, card numbers, or passwords
  • Your transaction history — it stays on your phone only
  • Your location
  • What you do inside the app or how you use it
  • Any information that could be used to identify or track you

You don't need to create an account or sign up. There is no cloud storage and no syncing.

💬

Why We Need Access to Your SMS

CoinDribble asks for permission to read your SMS messages so it can automatically detect bank payment alerts and add them to your spending history — saving you from entering transactions manually.

  • Only bank payment messages are looked at — personal messages are ignored
  • Your messages are read on your phone and are never sent anywhere
  • Only the amount, transaction type, and merchant name are saved — the original SMS is not stored
  • You can turn off this permission anytime in your phone's Settings → Apps → CoinDribble → Permissions
Your SMS messages stay on your phone at all times. CoinDribble has no internet access and cannot send anything anywhere.
🔐

How Your Data Is Stored & Protected

All your transactions, budgets, and settings are saved only on your phone in a secure, locked database.

  • Your data is locked with a strong security key that only your phone holds
  • Nobody — including TechAdhoc — can access your data
  • The app does not need the internet to work
  • If you uninstall the app, all your data is permanently deleted
💾

Backing Up Your Data

CoinDribble lets you save a backup of your transaction history to a file whenever you choose — for example, to your phone storage or Google Drive.

  • The backup file is locked with a password that you set
  • Nobody can open the backup without your password — we don't have it
  • You choose where to save the backup — it's entirely in your hands
  • The app never backs up or uploads your data automatically
📢

Ads in the App

CoinDribble shows a small number of simple ads through Google to help keep the app free. These ads are not based on your spending or financial activity.

  • Your money information and transactions are never shared with advertisers
  • Ads are not chosen based on what you do in the app
  • Google shows ads based on general, non-personal information

Google has its own privacy rules for the ads it shows. You can read more at Google's Privacy Policy .

🐛

When the App Crashes

If CoinDribble ever crashes or stops working, we automatically receive a short error report so we can find and fix the problem.

  • The report only tells us what went wrong technically — things like your phone model, Android version, and which part of the app crashed
  • Your financial data, transactions, SMS messages, and personal information are never included
  • These reports are used only to improve the app and fix bugs
  • The information is never used for ads and is never sold or shared
Crash reports help us fix issues faster — your financial data is never part of any report.
🔗

Other Services We Use

CoinDribble uses two outside services to help run the app:

  • Google Ads — to show simple ads that keep the app free; Google's Privacy Policy
  • Crash reporting — to automatically alert us if something breaks, so we can fix it quickly; no financial or personal data is included

We don't use any other outside services. Nothing that tracks your habits, reads your financial information, or requires you to log in.

Children

CoinDribble is intended for adults and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you think a child has used this app and shared any information, please email us at info@techadhoc.com and we'll take care of it right away.
